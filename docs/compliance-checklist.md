# Nepal Commercial & Legal Readiness Checklist

Cite official sources before sign-off; items below are prompts, not legal advice.

1. **Company registration** — Companies Act 2063 (or small-business form); ward business registration. Evidence: registration certificate number on file.
2. **PAN mandatory; VAT 13%** once turnover threshold crossed (IRD) — then VAT-inclusive pricing + monthly returns + VAT invoices.
3. **E-Commerce Act 2081** — check DOICS (Banijya Bibhag) listing/registration duty for online sellers; publish business name, reg. + PAN/VAT nos., address, grievance contacts, listing no.; update changes in prescribed period. (TO-VERIFY current rules.)
4. **Consumer Protection Act 2075** — accurate listings, tax-inclusive prices, delivery timelines, warranty info, return/refund policy, prompt invoices, grievance channel.
5. **Electronic Transactions Act 2063** — electronic records/signatures validity; data-privacy duties (consent, minimal retention, secure storage); publish Privacy Policy + cookie notice.
6. **Published pages** (all exist except Privacy/Terms/Shipping): Terms & Conditions (MISSING — add), Privacy Policy (MISSING — add), Return & Refund (exists `/return-policy`), Shipping/Delivery Policy (MISSING — add), About/Contact with real address + phone + map + response SLA.
7. **Payments** — hosted gateways only (no card storage); sandbox-tested Khalti/eSewa incl. failure/duplicate; secrets server-side; RLS verified in prod; admin audit log (`activity_logs`); DB backups enabled.
8. **Operations** — support chat/phone/email with hours; domain email (not Gmail); verified socials; Google Business Profile; zone/charge/cutoff table published; COD limits/fraud rules.
9. **Content/IP** — brand/logo trademark check; replace non-representative stock photos with real product photography (consumer-protection risk); model permissions.
10. **Accessibility/content** — WCAG AA spot pass; no dark patterns (fake urgency, fake discounts).
